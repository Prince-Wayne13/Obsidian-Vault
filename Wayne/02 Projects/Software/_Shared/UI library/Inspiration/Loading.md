# Loading

One finished loading screen and one reference sheet of loader animations.

## 1. Loading screen

![[Loading - the animation and content placement.jpg|420]]

**What I love:** the animation and content placement

**What it is:** A centred "Please wait a moment" screen: "Processing and analyzing your data..."

**How it's built:** Pale blue to white vertical gradient. A solid blue circle with a white bar-chart icon sits inside a pale ring, with a darker blue arc as the spinner and a thin outer ring. Bold heading and a grey one-line subtitle underneath.

**Rule to reuse:** Icon goes inside the spinner, text goes under it, everything centred. Say what is being done in the subtitle.

## 2. Loader sheet

![[loading - size and feel of icon, premium and professional modern, animationslook.jpg|420]]

**What I love:** size and feel of icon, premium and professional modern, animationslook

**What it is:** A dark reference sheet of loader animations in eight-tile rows: circle, dots, fade circles, ball, time, wifi and icons.

**How it's built:** White line icons on black rounded-square tiles on a dark grey board. Each tile in a row is a frame or variant of the same loader. The icon fills roughly half the tile.

**Rule to reuse:** Pick circle or dots for generic loading, and a themed loader (wifi, clock, download) only when it matches what is loading. Keep it monochrome and small.
