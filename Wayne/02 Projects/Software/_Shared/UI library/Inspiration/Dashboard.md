# Dashboard

You tagged these as dashboard. They are web page layouts (a projects grid, a services list and a hero), not app dashboards, so they are good for structure and typography rather than data widgets. Note: your file names for all three were cut off mid-sentence, so the "What I love" lines below end where the names did.

## 1. Projects grid (LYNIQ)

![[dashboard - even thought theres alot of content, its well structured not to overwhelm, colours are also minimal dula and give.jpg|420]]co.jpg|420]]alot of content, its well structured not to overwhelm, colours are also minimal dula and give modern low exposure low co

**What it is:** A white portfolio page: a huge bold "Projects" heading, short description on the right, text filter tabs, then a two-column grid of project cards.

**How it's built:** Heading is very large and tight; the description is tiny grey text aligned to the right. Filter tabs are plain text (active one black, the rest grey) over a hairline. Cards are an image, a medium title, one grey line and tiny outlined tag pills. Small red corner marks sit on each image.

**Rule to reuse:** Huge heading, tiny supporting text. Two columns with generous gaps. Tags are tiny outlined pills. Greyscale everywhere, with a single red accent on the corner marks.

## 2. Services list (dark)

![[dashboard - the line demacations , the big font content and small font pairing and structure, the.jpg|420]]

**What I love:** the line demacations , the big font content and small font pairing and structure, the

**What it is:** A black page with a thin white frame, an intro statement and four numbered service rows.

**How it's built:** Rows are separated by hairline rules. Each row has a small italic serif number (/01), a large light sans title in the middle, tiny body text on the right and a micro "VIEW —— PROJECTS" link. The intro paragraph dims one phrase.

**Rule to reuse:** Use hairlines instead of boxes to divide content. Big title next to tiny text. A serif italic for numbers gives contrast against the geometric sans.

## 3. Hero with feature cards (Furnizen)

**What I love:** the placement of componets doesnt feel overwhelming, blend of colours is top, cards have like two contents ans are perfect, content formatti

**What it is:** A furniture hero: headline and buttons on the left, a large rounded photo on the right, a row of cards underneath.

**How it's built:** White page on a warm grey-brown backdrop. Bold dark headline, black pill "Shop Now" plus a text link. The bottom row has two beige-grey rounded cards, each holding two small features (icon, bold title, one short line), and a black rounded square with an arrow.

**Rule to reuse:** Cards hold two items each, so the row never feels crowded. Warm neutral palette with black as the only strong accent. Photo gets a large radius.
