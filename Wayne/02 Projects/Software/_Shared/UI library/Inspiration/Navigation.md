# Navigation

These two files are the same pictures as the first and third Dashboard images, filed under a second target. Only the top bars matter here.

## 1. Minimal text nav (LYNIQ)

![[navigation - font and colour blending and spacing.jpg|420]]

**What I love:** font and colour blending and spacing

**What it is:** Logo on the left, three tiny uppercase links spread evenly, and a MENU label with hamburger lines on the right. Below, text filter tabs over a hairline.

**How it's built:** Everything is small and black on white, with a lot of space between items. The active tab is black and the rest are grey.

**Rule to reuse:** Tiny uppercase links with wide spacing. Active state is only a colour change, with no pill or underline.

## 2. Nav with pill CTA (Furnizen)

![[Navigation - how it blends wiht the background.jpg|420]]

**What I love:** how it blends wiht the background

**What it is:** Logo left, five small links centred, black pill "Sign up" on the right, on a white card.

**How it's built:** Plain small dark links and no boxes, so the bar blends into the page. The only strong shape is the black pill.

**Rule to reuse:** Links stay unboxed and quiet, with one filled pill as the only call to action. See [[Pill nav]] for your existing version.
