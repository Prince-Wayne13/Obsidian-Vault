# Card

Two card layouts: a dominant banner over a row of small cards, and a stacked swap widget.

## 1. Banner over three cards

![[Card - the dominacne of the red card, perfect odd arrangement of cards below it, minimal content, soft icon and font.jpg|420]]

**What I love:** the dominacne of the red card, perfect odd arrangement of cards below it, minimal content, soft icon and font

**What it is:** A browser-framed page: one wide red-orange banner with a centred white headline, and three equal cards below it.

**How it's built:** Banner has a dark red to orange wave gradient and a large white headline. The three cards are white with a hairline border and a small orange-tinted icon tile, a bold title, two or three lines of tiny grey text and a "Get started" link pinned to the bottom.

**Rule to reuse:** One dominant colour block, everything else white and quiet. Tint the icon tiles with the banner colour. Keep card copy to a title, a short line and one link.

## 2. Swap widget

![[Card - the pill with swap its color combo and font, the style of two cards, the three nav like pills softness and backgroung blending.jpg|420]]

**What I love:** the pill with swap its color combo and font, the style of two cards, the three nav like pills softness and backgroung blending

**What it is:** A crypto swap card with Swap / Send / Buy tabs, two stacked amount cards and a big black Swap button.

**How it's built:** Tabs are small pills, the active one filled light grey. Sell and Buy are white rounded cards with a large number, small grey fiat value and a token chip (pill with icon and chevron). A white circle button with a down arrow overlaps the seam between them. The CTA is a full-width black pill with a soft shadow. Quiet grey page background.

**Rule to reuse:** Big number, small grey secondary text. Put the connector button on the seam between two cards. One black pill as the only strong element.
