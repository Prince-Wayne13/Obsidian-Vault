# Modal

Success and error modals that share one structure and change only colour.

## 1. Success and error modals

![[Modal - i love it all...i can play around with the colors depenidng on use, i even love the big iconimage.jpg|420]]

**What I love:** i love it all...i can play around with the colors depenidng on use, i even love the big iconimage

**What it is:** Two overlapping cards: an orange "Sorry :(" error and a blue "Success!" confirmation, over a slate background with a white wave.

**How it's built:** White cards with a large radius and a soft shadow. A line illustration with small sparkles on top, a coloured bold title, grey-navy body text and a full-width pill button in capitals (TRY AGAIN / OKAY). The success card has a grey round close button overlapping its top-right corner.

**Rule to reuse:** One accent colour per state drives the illustration, the title and the button. Change the accent, keep the layout. Use a large illustration at the top.
