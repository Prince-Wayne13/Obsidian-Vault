# Alert

Four different ways to tell the user something: inline status banners, a notification stack, a confirm dialog and an invite card.

## 1. Status banners

![[Alert - colour fill, border glassy.jpg|420]]

**What I love:** colour fill, border glassy

**What it is:** Four stacked inline alerts (success green, error red, warning amber, info blue).

**How it's built:** Pale tinted fill per status, a white border that reads as glass, a white circle behind a coloured icon on the left, bold title with a two-line grey body, thin X top-right.

**Rule to reuse:** One pale tint per status. Put the coloured icon on a white disc so it stays readable on any tint. White border, no dark outline.

## 2. Notification stack

![[Alert - for a playful website the rounded shapes are perfect, icon placement, title + content is perfect not too much.jpg|420]]

**What I love:** for a playful website the rounded shapes are perfect, icon placement, title + content is perfect not too much

**What it is:** A stack of notifications under a "View all notifications" heading, with a round bell button and a red count badge (8) below.

**How it's built:** Fully rounded pill cards in off-white with a soft shadow. Each has a tinted circular icon badge on the left (blue, orange, purple, amber), a semibold title and one grey detail line. Each type gets its own icon colour.

**Rule to reuse:** Title plus one line of detail, nothing more. Icon colour tells the type, the card stays neutral. Bell with badge is the entry point.

## 3. Delete confirm dialog

![[Alert - perfectly rounded, font size, icon placement, shadow.jpg|420]]

**What I love:** perfectly rounded, font size, icon placement, shadow

**What it is:** A centred confirmation dialog: "Delete this item?" with Cancel and Delete.

**How it's built:** White card with moderate radius and a large soft shadow. Pink circle with a red outlined warning icon at the top, centred title, two lines of body, then two equal-width buttons: outlined Cancel and a muted red filled Delete. Geometric sans (Poppins-like).

**Rule to reuse:** Destructive action is a muted red, not a harsh one. Both buttons same width, cancel outlined, icon centred above the title.

## 4. Invite card with actions

![[Alert - pills are orefctly layered with the main card, boarder radius is perfect, the blend of bold title and font size.jpg|420]]

**What I love:** pills are orefctly layered with the main card, boarder radius is perfect, the blend of bold title and font size

**What it is:** A floating "Invite your friends" card with two actions, sitting at the edge of a larger white panel.

**How it's built:** White card with a hairline border and a big radius. Grey rounded-square icon tile, bold title, grey body, X top-right. A dashed divider separates the body from two side-by-side buttons: outlined "Share via Email" and dark filled "Copy Link" with an icon.

**Rule to reuse:** Dashed divider between message and actions. One outlined and one dark filled button, same height, the dark one is the main action.
