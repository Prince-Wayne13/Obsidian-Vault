# T-Tech UI: first three pages (updated 27 November 2026)

Home, Get a quote and One quote as a front-end preview (React, Tailwind v4, sample data, no backend).

- Preview: https://claude.ai/artifact/SmazRAtQ9XMjthLjEMHu5g (private until shared)

## Current design (after review feedback: calmer, merge Midday with tetris)
- Look: Styles/Midday from the vault (parchment, white sheets, Hedvig serif and sans, pill buttons, flat) with tetris-style cut-out corners.
- Colour: warm neutrals; T-Tech teal #246792 is the only accent; dark frames use #0C1A1E. Still to be confirmed against the source artwork.
- Home: centred headline, carousel in a dark frame, four order steps as stepped cut-out pieces, talk to staff.
- Get a quote: step list that locks into one sheet; one step at a time.
- One quote: single column paper quote with a tear-off total, then payment, then questions and changes.
- Dynamic components (marked in the preview with dashed teal outlines): DepthCarousel (Home, past work pop-up), SpotlightCard (quote total, message topics), VoicePill (voice notes), SwipeToast (confirmations).
- Also used: PillNav, Hero, CutoutCard. FlipCard was removed from the pages.
- Revive follows the newer rule (Checking until staff check the price).
