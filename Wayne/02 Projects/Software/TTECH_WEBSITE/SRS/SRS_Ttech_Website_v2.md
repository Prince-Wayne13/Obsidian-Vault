## How the website and WhatsApp work together

This rule keeps the work simple for staff and keeps one clear record of every job.

- **The website is the official record.** Quote requests, quotes, acceptance, files, payments and job progress all live on the website.
- **WhatsApp is for talking and reminders.** Staff never need to type the same thing twice.
- **Quote steps:** (1) The customer builds and sends a quote request on the website. (2) The website emails the customer to say it arrived. The shop's WhatsApp Business number also sends its automatic greeting message. (3) Staff write the quote once, on the website. (4) The website emails the quote as a link. Staff send the same link on WhatsApp. (5) The customer opens the link, reads the quote, and ticks the agreement to the terms. (6) The customer pays the 50% deposit and uploads proof, or pays cash at the shop. (7) The customer presses OK. (8) Staff approve the payment, and the order is created.
- **Proofs (a picture of how the finished print will look) at launch:** the designer sends the proof on WhatsApp. The customer replies "OK" on WhatsApp. Staff save that reply and attach it to the order on the website. Approving proofs on the website itself comes later.
- **Reminders at launch:** staff send reminders on WhatsApp by hand. Automatic reminders come later.

## 1. Purpose

A modern, mobile-friendly website that helps the business attract customers, display print services clearly, receive and manage orders, and reduce back-and-forth on WhatsApp, calls, and email.

It serves retail customers (individuals) and business customers (companies, schools, churches, NGOs, shops, institutions). At launch, business customers are treated like all other customers.

The main aim is to turn visitors into paying customers by making it easy to understand our services, ask for a price, upload print files, approve artwork, and track jobs.

## 11. Artwork and Order Policy

The website must show these terms before a customer accepts a quote:

- Customers are responsible for checking spelling, sizes, colours and content before approval.
- Every order needs a 50% deposit. Production begins only after the deposit is approved and artwork is approved, unless agreed otherwise.
- The other 50% can be paid at any time, but the job cannot be collected until it is paid in full.
- If a file is unsuitable, we will ask the customer for a better one at no charge. If the customer wants us to design or redo the artwork, staff will price that work.
- Colours on a screen may look different from the printed result.
- Low-quality pictures may print badly.
- Turnaround time starts after artwork approval and deposit approval.
- We may refuse files that are unsuitable, unlawful, offensive, or copy someone else's work.
- What happens to a customer's money if they cancel after paying or after artwork approval will be added once the business decides it.
