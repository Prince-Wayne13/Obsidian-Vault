
### Design Objectives
1. Correctness
	- Does it satisfy all functional and non functinal req
	- is it feasible with constraints
	- **Did you solve the  *right* problem and adhered strictly to the constraints**
2. Efficiency
	- How well is it using its resources- processor, memory, storaege
3. Maintanability
	- Ease of testing
	- ease of discovering bugs
	- ease of fixing a fault
	- ease of adding new features
	- **Depends on architectural design -**
4. Cost
